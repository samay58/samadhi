# Glass transport circles

Fresh frames for the transport and Finish controls after the glass capsule bar was replaced by three separate interactive Liquid Glass circles. Captured on the iPhone 17 Pro Simulator, iOS 27, from the full serial interface test run on 2026-08-19. Demo playlists only; no private music.

- `after-transport-normal.png`: 68 point clear glass circles for Previous and Next, an 88 point ivory-tinted circle with an ink glyph for Pause, Finish hairline pill below.
- `after-transport-paused-normal.jpg`: the same row paused; the primary circle holds `play.fill`.
- `after-finish-armed-normal.png`: Finish armed; the row dims to about a third and `Hold to finish` replaces the word in place.
- `after-finish-cancelled-normal.jpg`: after an early release, the run continues and the fill has reset.
- `after-transport-*` and `after-finish-armed-*` at accessibility XXXL, Reduce Motion, Increased Contrast, and Reduced Transparency.

What these frames prove: layout, proportion, and the four environment variants. What they do not prove: the glass lensing on real content, the spring press, haptic character, or daylight legibility, all of which are physical.
